It’s been a couple of weeks since I announced that I got my amateur radio license and wanted to provide an update on what I’ve been doing since then.

Got my First Radio!

Broadly speaking (and according to my limited understanding), there are three types of amateur transceivers, or radios:

  • Handheld transceivers (HTs), also known as “handie-talkies”. These look like the small, handheld “walkie-talkies” you may have seen in the big box retailers or online. HTs use a small rechargeable battery, come with a “rubber duck” antenna and are usually limited to between 5 and 10W of power. They are generally inexpensive, though you can easily find models that cost hundreds of dollars. They make a good first radio for Technician-class operators because they generally operate on the dual bands of 2m and 70cm (VHF/UHF) for which Technicians have operating privileges.
