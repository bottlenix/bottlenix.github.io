Install the Mumble Client on your Mac or PC

To install the Mumble client on your Mac or PC, go to the Downloads page and select either the "Windows 64-bit" or the "macOS" version. Install the application.

If you are installing Mumble on a Mac, you need to allow your Mac to read certain key presses to enable push-to-talk. Push to talk requires you to push a key when you want to speak. On the Mac, go to the "Settings" app and then go to "Security & Privacy" and then click the "Privacy" tab. Click the lock icon on the bottom left to make changes and then select "Accessibility" from the left hand side and click the box next to "Mumble.app".

Open the application and go through the Audio Wizard setup. This will ensure that Mumble operates with the best audio quality for your setup and internet speed. For the most part, you can just accept the defaults.

On the next screen ("Voice Activity Detection"), you can figure the "Push to Talk" setting. This allows you to select a key to press and hold whenever you want to speak. For example, on a PC, you can select the Right key. On a Mac, you can select the <alt/option> key as an example. Select a key that you do not typically use.

Go through the remaining screens to complete the Audio Wizard configuration.

From the "Mumble Server Connect" window, click "Add New...".

Then provide the following details in the "Add Server" screen:

  • Address: talk.pokerlounge.net
  • Port: 64927
  • Username: choose a username
  • Label: poker or some other description

Connect to the new server by selecting it and then clicking "Connect".

If you get a warning asking you about an untrusted certificate, go ahead and Accept the certificate (or ignore it, depending on what you are asked).

Finally, enter the room's password.

You should now be connected to the server.

Remember that in order to talk, you must first press and hold the key you selected before. Otherwise, you will be on mute by default. The lips on the mouth icon should turn red when you are unmuted indicating that you will be heard by others when speaking.
